# Deny authorization prompts to the guarded desktop

## Description

OpenCode Desktop's updater (electron-updater with Squirrel.Mac) checks for updates at launch and every 10 minutes, and retries after a failure. Inside the guard it cannot replace the app bundle, so it asked for an administrator password through Authorization Services. Observed with 2.1.1 staging 2.1.4 on 2026-10-24: the cancel was logged as `OSStatus error -60006`. The profile does not restrict `mach-lookup`, so guarded desktop processes can reach `com.apple.authd` and raise administrator prompts. Denying that lookup in GUI mode makes the update fail without a prompt.

## Scope / Surfaces Affected

- `engine/profile.sb`: the GUI-only block.
- `test/fixtures/differences/`: a new step for the golden comparison.
- A Seatbelt launch check for the deny.
- `SECURITY.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md` and `docs/THREAT-MODEL.md`, where profile rules are listed.
- Out of scope:
  - the CLI profile (`GUI=0`) and the Pi profile;
  - updater configuration and `OPENCODE_DISABLE_AUTOUPDATE`;
  - user guidance in `docs/USAGE.md`, which a separate item covers.

## Acceptance Criteria

- With `GUI=1`, a process under the profile cannot look up `com.apple.authd`, and Authorization Services calls fail without any UI.
- The `GUI=0` profile is unchanged.
- `test/golden.mjs` records the change as a new difference step.
- No test raises an authorization or privacy prompt.

## How to Verify

- A Seatbelt check runs `security authorize` without interaction under `GUI=1` and `GUI=0`, and distinguishes the denied lookup from the normal "interaction not allowed" result.
- `node test/golden.mjs` passes.
- The hosted `tests` check passes.
