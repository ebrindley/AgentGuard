# Resolve Pi checker gaps before switching to cc-safety-net

## Description

Step 7e of docs/DESIGN.md: before Pi moves to cc-safety-net at step 10d, measure both checkers on the same commands and give every difference a disposition. The comparison runner and fixture shipped in #131 (`test/checker-comparison.mjs`, `test/fixtures/checker-comparison/`, docs/CHECKER-COMPARISON.md), with 473 cases: 414 characterization, 34 targeted and 25 benign. Its conclusion is to keep Pi's current checker. This item covers the dispositions and environments that comparison left open.

## Scope / Surfaces Affected

- The open groups in docs/CHECKER-COMPARISON.md, Bounded policy dispositions:
  - Remote history, local discard and hooks: Pi's ASK for force, delete, mirror, prune and lease pushes, destructive reset and clean, and `core.hooksPath` changes. The supported `cc-safety-net/api` returns only `allow` or `deny`, so preserving ASK needs a supported matching interface or a second ask-list evaluation.
  - Pipe to shell: the severity of `pipe-shell` and `pipe-shell-chain`.
  - Catastrophic recursive targets: an in-scope coverage map before any Pi coverage is removed, and the Node and Ruby root-API behavior.
  - Secret paths: the shared checker's refusals of public `.pem` fixtures and data heredocs.
  - Wrapper failure behavior: bounded-cost evidence for in-process checking.
- Whether cc-safety-net's Pi entry loads in OMP, in a disposable home.
- Latency per call for both checkers.
- Out of scope: the routine-cleanup lexical-root fix, which is a separate follow-up; a new parser; importing private cc-safety-net exports or forking the vendor.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every open group has a disposition: denied by Seatbelt, out of scope (evasion), or in scope with a rule, an ask-list entry, an upstream issue or a written acceptance.
- OMP's load result is recorded.
- p50 and p95 latency for both checkers, and the count of 2-second timeouts.
- The fixture is regenerated with any changed verdicts, and D5's done-when in docs/DESIGN.md is met or the item records why Pi keeps its checker.

## How to Verify

- The comparison script reproduces the fixture.
- Optional owner-provided aggregate ask/block counts may inform cost. Agents do not read the raw security log. Zero hits do not prove a protection redundant.
