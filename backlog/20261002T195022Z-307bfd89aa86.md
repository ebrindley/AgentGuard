# Move Pi and OMP to cc-safety-net

## Description

Pi and OMP use pi-sandbox-guard's analyzer, about 5,500 lines of bash and JavaScript maintained here, while OpenCode uses cc-safety-net. Move Pi and OMP to cc-safety-net with the checker policy of docs/DESIGN.md decision D13, and retire the analyzer (section 12, step 10d). cc-safety-net returns only allow or deny, so Pi's confirmations become one or the other, as THREAT-MODEL.md section 8 records.

## Scope / Surfaces Affected

- The Pi extension: run cc-safety-net's Pi entry through a wrapper with Agent Guard's rulebook, in place of the analyzer.
- cc-safety-net user-level policy and Agent Guard's rulebook: deny plain force pushes, mirror pushes, force-pushed tags, `core.hooksPath` writes and whole-tree discards; allow `--force-with-lease`, remote branch deletion and path-scoped discards (D13).
- The analyzer, its interpreter binding (`.guard-node`, `--checker-node`) and its log: retired.
- `test/checker-comparison.mjs` and its fixture: regenerated against the new policy.
- Release notes: the confirmations removed and the commands newly refused.
- Out of scope: a new parser, private cc-safety-net exports or a vendor fork.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every command group of docs/CHECKER-COMPARISON.md has its D13 verdict, or a recorded reason where cc-safety-net cannot express it.
- If cc-safety-net cannot tell path-scoped discards from whole-tree ones, its default deny of all local discards stays, and the item records it.
- cc-safety-net's Pi entry loads in OMP, in a disposable home.
- p50 and p95 latency per call and the count of 2-second timeouts are recorded.
- A degraded cc-safety-net policy or a missing rulebook blocks the call.
- The analyzer and its binding are gone from the release, and uninstall and update handle installs that still have them.

## How to Verify

- The comparison script reproduces the fixture.
- Real Pi and OMP sessions in a disposable home: a denied and an allowed command from each group.
